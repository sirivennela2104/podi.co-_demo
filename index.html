Made slowly, in small batches

Every batch is dry-roasted, cooled and ground in small lots so the aroma stays in the jar, not in the factory.

We start with lentils, sesame and whole spices, roast each one separately and grind coarse. There are no preservatives, no colours and no fillers. Open a jar and you can smell the difference.

Three ways to enjoy

Simple, quick and very South Indian.

  1. Classic: mix a spoon of podi with ghee or sesame oil and eat with hot idli or dosa.
  2. Rice bowl: stir into steaming rice with a drop of ghee and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Sprinkle: dust over roasted vegetables, eggs or curd rice for a quick kick.
